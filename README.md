# InfiniteTinkerer

**The Infinite Toking Tinkerer · Φ369 Labs**

A playful, free and open-source home for **Lil' Phi Guy**, **Pieces of Phi**, the **Infinite Quarter** retro micro-adventures, and **Φ Content Lab**.

> One coin. Infinite possibilities. **Face value: 1 REPO.**

## The idea

A green-and-gold quarter featuring the Infinite Toking Tinkerer goes into jukeboxes, payphones, arcade machines and other old-school devices. A Gen.E-Wrist, with a very suspicious string attached, always retrieves it. Each little adventure can reveal original music, a working project, or a creative experiment.

People earn **Pieces of Phi** for real milestones in things they make. Three pieces for *Imagine*, six for *Build*, nine for *Share*: 18 pieces forge one symbolic **Lil' Phi Guy / 1 REPO** achievement. The pieces are **not currency, crypto, or a financial instrument**.

Φ Content Lab provides a local-first workspace for drafting authentic educational posts, curating communities, planning timing experiments, and measuring results. It **does not scrape Facebook, auto-post to groups, generate fake engagement, or promise monetization**.

## Getting started

This repository is being initialized. See the first feature PR for the browser-based interactive prototype.

## Principles

- MIT licensed, free to fork and adapt.
- No paid services required and no backend by default.
- Human-reviewed publishing and genuine community participation.
- Original creative work and credited music/assets only.
- Local-first data and transparent measurements, never invented reach or revenue.
- Integration over duplication: link to Gen.E-Wrist, PhiOS, FieldConstellation, and other existing projects rather than copying them.

## Planned experiences

1. **Lil' Phi Guy:** interactive quarter, string gag, 1 REPO badge.
2. **Retro Adventures:** jukebox, payphone, endless kitchen-phone cord, cosmic switchboard.
3. **Pieces of Phi:** project milestones, collectible pieces, playful completion animation.
4. **Φ Content Lab:** editorial drafts, manual group research, timing and performance experiments.
5. **Music and media:** opt-in use of original user-supplied tracks and approved coin art.
